Terms of Service

Boulder Black Line · Last updated October 28, 2026

These terms describe how Boulder Black Line takes and runs bookings. By booking a ride with us, you agree to these terms.

The service

Boulder Black Line provides private rides by reservation in the Denver metro, in one premium black SUV with one driver. Rides are first booked, first served.

Bookings

A booking made on this site, by phone, or by text is a request. Your ride is booked once we confirm it by email or text. Please give a working mobile number and email address so we can reach you about your ride, including a reminder before pickup and updates while your driver is on the way.

Rates

Rates are flat and shown before you book. The rate in your confirmation is the rate for your ride.

Payment

You pay your driver directly at the ride, by card or cash. Nothing is charged when you book.

Airport pickups

For airport pickups, we track your flight. Collect your bags, come out of the terminal, and meet your driver at Level 5, Island 2, on the West or East side you picked when you booked.

Changes and cancellations

To change or cancel a ride, call or text us at (720) 326-4652 as early as you can so we can help.
